**USIPE Office Actions v1.0.0 is in OpenAI Platform review, not confirmed published.** [PR #147](https://github.com/jabbertones-cloud/USIPEorg/pull/147) documents two tool definitions flagged for further review and a proposed policy change. ## Separate Office Action assistance from proactive opposition research

- **Office Actions:** USIPE's intended workflows include USPTO-record analysis, structured response research and deadline assistance. Always verify current deadlines and filing records against official USPTO sources. A software estimate does not file a response, buy an extension or substitute for qualified counsel.
- **Earlier enforcement signals:** [USIPEorg PR #146](https://github.com/jabbertones-cloud/USIPEorg/pull/146) contains **unmerged, source-labeled** Gazette look-ahead and historical cease-and-desist chronology research. A human-review candidate is **not** proof of an upcoming demand, opposition or objection.
- **Outcome learning:** Dated official TTAB pleadings and dispositions can help investigate repeated filing patterns. Distinguish filing sequence from proof that an Answer caused dismissal or that a case ended without a private settlement.
- **Outreach:** [PR #142](https://github.com/jabbertones-cloud/USIPEorg/pull/142) and [PR #143](https://github.com/jabbertones-cloud/USIPEorg/pull/143) address provider acceptance versus verified email delivery. Those changes were not established as released by this review. Do not state “recipient warned” from a queue/accepted API response alone.

## Troubleshoot and report safely

Use the [troubleshooting guide](troubleshooting.md) and [FAQ](faq.md), then [search public issues](https://github.com/jabbertones-cloud/usipe-support/issues) or [file a public bug](https://github.com/jabbertones-cloud/usipe-support/issues/new/choose). Include only public, sanitized source links, the tool/client/version, and reproducible behavior. Do **not** post confidential client correspondence, unpublished strategy, private letters, authentication secrets or matter details.

Official status and evidence should come from [USPTO Trademark Status and Document Retrieval](https://tsdr.uspto.gov/), [TTABVUE](https://ttabvue.uspto.gov/) and the applicable USPTO rules, not from a model's prediction. USIPE is not the USPTO or a law firm.
